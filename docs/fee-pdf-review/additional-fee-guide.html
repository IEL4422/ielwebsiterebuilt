Uncontested guardianship

Opening, ordinary administration and closing are included in the retained scope. Annual reporting is the only additional firm-fee category for ordinary uncontested guardianship.

Annual Report on the Ward$750per year it is prepared and filed

Preparation and filing of the personal report, separate from the initial guardianship fee.

For ordinary uncontested guardianship, annual reporting is the only additional firm fee. Routine administration, closing and termination within the retained scope are included. The Annual Report on the Ward costs $750 per year for preparation and filing. A later contested proceeding requires its own specifically accepted fixed-fee addendum.

Your scope and fee protections

  • Every additional service needs a stated scope, exact fixed fee and your specific acceptance of an addendum before a charge. A general acknowledgment does not authorize future charges.
  • Required court approval remains separate; this guide does not authorize payment from estate or ward funds.
  • Settlement completes the defined retained work. If representation ends before that work is complete, any refund reflects work performed, subject to mandatory reasonableness, refund and court-review requirements.
  • Existing signed agreements and previously included expenses remain protected. This prospective guide does not impose retroactive charges.
